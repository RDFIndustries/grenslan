Grenslan runs SearXNG as a separately managed process and communicates with
it only through its HTTP interface. Grenslan does not incorporate SearXNG
source code.

Bundled build: commit df67eb6c2608c5b2f909ed7c42b0a4bd3823f78f (version 2026.9.21+df67eb6c2),
from https://github.com/searxng/searxng.

Corresponding source: the complete source of the bundled build, including
its LICENSE file, is installed with it, in the src directory of the
SearXNG installation (for example <install_prefix>/src). It is also
available from the repository above at that commit. packaging/searxng/ in
the Grenslan source records how it is installed.
